Accounts and profile

The game creates an anonymous account for progress and multiplayer. We process player IDs, friend codes, session credentials, nicknames, selected avatars and cosmetics. Email and password are not required for guest play. Optional profile fields include gender, birthday month/day, region and a signature. Region is entered by you, not obtained through GPS. Public profile information is visible to other players.

Game data and diagnostics

We process progress, match and ranking records, virtual-item and currency transactions, mail claims, friends, rooms and online status. To operate and improve the game we process activity times, tutorial and gameplay events, frame rate, errors, game version, device model, OS, GPU, screen size, processor count, language and random installation/session IDs. Network requests include IP addresses, times and connection information for connectivity, security, rate limiting and troubleshooting.

Text and voice

Text messages and sender IDs are delivered to their selected recipients or channels. Private chat normally keeps the latest 200 messages per friend pair; after friendship removal, related history is retained for up to 30 days. World chat normally retains messages for 7 days. Reports may preserve messages, profile snapshots and investigation records beyond ordinary retention.

Microphone access is optional. Live voice is encrypted in transit to our LiveKit server and forwarded to the participants shown in the interface. We do not record or store live calls. This is transport encryption, not a claim of end-to-end encryption. Voice-room and connection logs may contain player IDs and join/leave times.

Recorded voice messages are different from live calls. When you record and send a message, up to 30 seconds of audio is uploaded, transcoded and stored for recipients to play. Private voice messages normally expire after 30 days; world and room/match messages after 7 days. Reported audio is held for at least 60 days and may be retained longer while investigated. Periodic cleanup and references can delay physical file deletion. Audio is delivered through content-hash URLs; someone who obtains a URL may access its audio. Do not send sensitive information. Recording is not required to play.

Providers, advertising and international processing

We use Google Cloud, Supabase, Firebase and our own LiveKit services for hosting, authentication, storage and delivery. Data may be processed outside your country. We do not sell personal information or use chat or voice for advertising. The current release has no ads or real-money purchases. Distribution platforms process their own account and download data under their policies.

Retention and deletion

Account and progress data are kept while providing service. Logs rotate; there is no single fixed retention period for all logs. Match, virtual-item, penalty and report records may be retained long-term for support, disputes and abuse prevention.

In the player profile choose Delete Account / 注销账号 and confirm your friend code. This removes profile, friend and ordinary chat data, related activity/analytics records and display information, and makes the old account unrecoverable. Some match, transaction, safety and report records may remain, including copied report evidence and anonymous technical authentication records. Retained data is not necessarily irreversibly anonymized. You can also email support to request deletion, access or correction; we verify account ownership and explain any remaining records. Never send passwords or tokens.

Your choices and minors

You may avoid chat, decline microphone access, mute players, report abuse, restrict supported profile fields or delete your account. Uninstalling does not delete server data. The game is not directed to children under 13; follow higher local or platform age requirements. We use encryption and access controls but cannot guarantee absolute security. Material changes will be reflected in this policy and appropriate notices.
